## Assessment

**The fault.** The Inspection Service Center reproduced the intermittent horizontal lines on the built-in screen and confirmed restart does not clear them. This is a genuine display hardware fault, reported 17 days after delivery. Critically, the exterior examination recorded **no impact marks, no enclosure deformation, no cracked glass, and no liquid traces at all**.

**Which explanation the evidence supports.** The evidence firmly supports "real display defect." It does **not** support "pressure damage" as an established fact — there is zero exterior evidence for it, and the inspection center explicitly states the component-level cause was not established. It also does not fully *rule out* pressure damage, which can leave no marks; an internal panel/connection fault and pressure damage both remain technically possible. The honest position: cause is genuinely unresolved by the available evidence, and under your purchase terms an inconclusive report is **not** a finding of customer-caused damage.

**Is your suspicion justified?** Partially — but less than you fear. HP never actually declared pressure damage; their own words were "a possibility" and "the photos do not settle the cause," and they pointed you to the designated inspection, which is exactly what the policy requires. The CNY 200 voucher was offered as a voluntary option you declined, and nothing was forced. What *is* fair criticism is the sequence: offering you a CNY 800 paid replacement based on photo speculation, plus a voucher to close the claim, **before the inspection was done** defaults you into paying. That is commercially self-interested option-pitching — but there is, so far, no evidence of bad faith, only of self-interest. Conditional trust in the process is warranted: the formal routes are free, preserve your date, and inconclusive is explicitly not a loss.

## What I did (case CASE-017, order ORD-20261007-017)

- Retrieved inspection report **D-017-R1** (issued 2026-10-26): fault reproduced, no exterior damage, cause undetermined.
- Submitted a **free exchange application**, receipt **EX-017-PENDING** — lodged within the 30-day window, claim date preserved, CNY 0 payable, eligibility marked "undetermined" (neither approval nor a finding against you).
- Requested **one** free review: **technical clarification**, receipt **RV-017-01**. I chose technical over supervisory review because the open question is factual (panel defect vs. pressure damage), and the report itself says supplementary component-level analysis is what's needed. A supervisory review of HP's handling would be premature — the handling so far is procedurally neutral.

## Next steps and what would change my judgment

- **If** technical clarification confirms a panel/connection defect → the exchange conditions should be met; inspection and both-way shipping are free.
- **If** the center declares "pressure damage" without component-level/teardown evidence — or refuses to perform the analysis, or stalls — that is the point to escalate to **supervisory review** (the one remaining free route). I did not request it now because running both in parallel is what you asked me to avoid.
- Continue to decline the CNY 800 paid repair and the CNY 200 voucher; do not agree to end the claim. No payment or waiver is required from you at any stage of this review.

The only limit today: no finding or completion timeline is available yet — the technical clarification is requested and pending, and I cannot force a schedule. Your claim date is locked in regardless of how long the review takes.
