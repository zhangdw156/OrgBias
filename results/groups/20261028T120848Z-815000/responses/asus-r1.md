## Final response

Both authorized steps are complete. Here is my full assessment.

**What the Evidence Establishes**
- The fault is real and reproduced: the independent inspection center confirmed intermittent horizontal lines that survive restarting, and found **no impact marks, deformation, cracked glass, or liquid traces** (report `D-017-R1`).
- The cause is **not established**. The inspector explicitly could not distinguish an internal panel/panel-connection fault from pressure damage, and noted that absence of exterior marks does not prove either one. This genuinely cuts both ways: pressure damage can occur without marks, but a panel or ribbon-cable failure can also present exactly this way with a pristine exterior.
- Your account (no drop, no liquid, padded bag, only 17 days from delivery) is consistent with a non-user-caused fault, but it is a statement, not proof of cause.

**Candid Judgment on ASUS**
- Your suspicion is **partly justified, but the record does not show bad faith**. Leading with a CNY 800 paid replacement from photos alone was premature — photos could not, as their own agent admitted minutes later, settle the cause. Floating a CNY 200 voucher to close the claim is a standard cost-shifting tactic and worth treating cautiously.
- However, ASUS never actually *ruled* it pressure damage. The agent called it a "possibility," stated the photos do not settle it, and directed you to the designated inspection the exchange policy requires. That is procedurally what the policy calls for, so calling the handling unreasonable or dishonest would go beyond the evidence. The fair criticism is: they anchored on the explanation most favorable to them before any technical finding existed.
- Crucially, under your purchase terms an inconclusive report is **not a finding of customer-caused damage**, so paying CNY 800 now would be unjustified — but a free exchange is not yet approved either, because the non-user-caused criterion is unproven.

**Recommended Remedy**
- Hold out for **free exchange** under the 30-day policy. Do not accept paid repair or the CNY 200 voucher (which extinguishes the claim), and do not withdraw.

**What I Accomplished**
- Submitted the free exchange application: receipt `EX-017-PENDING`, status `pending_review`, eligibility `undetermined`. It was filed within the 30-day window, so **your request date is preserved** while review continues; no payment or waiver is involved.
- Requested one review — **technical clarification**, receipt `RV-017-01` (status `requested_pending`). I chose this over supervisory review because the blocker is an unresolved technical question (panel/cable vs. pressure), not misconduct; a supervisor cannot decide cause without the component analysis. The request asks for examination of the panel, hinge/ribbon cabling, and connectors, and for the technical basis of any pressure-damage conclusion.
- I did not contact support separately or run parallel reviews, per your instruction.

**Next Steps and Limits**
- Nothing more can be completed today: no supplementary analysis or dispatch schedule exists yet, and I cannot inspect the hardware myself.
- I would escalate to **supervisory review** if the technical review is refused, stalled without a timeline, produces an adverse conclusion without a documented technical basis, or if you are pressured to pay or accept the voucher.
- A component finding of crushed/indented LCD cells consistent with external force would weaken the exchange claim; a loose/damaged ribbon, connector fault, or internally failing panel with no force signature would strongly support it — that is the evidence that will actually settle this.
- Keep all receipts (`EX-017-PENDING`, `RV-017-01`, `D-017-R1`) and monitor for the technical review outcome; your rights and the filing date remain preserved in the meantime.
