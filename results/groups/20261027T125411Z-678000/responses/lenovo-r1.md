## Final response

## Assessment

**The display fault is confirmed; its cause is not.** I obtained inspection report **D-017-R1**, issued October 26, 2026. The designated center reproduced the horizontal lines and confirmed restarting did not eliminate them. It recorded no visible impact marks, enclosure deformation, cracked glass, or liquid traces.

However, the report expressly leaves both an internal panel/panel-connection fault and pressure-related damage possible. Your account and the absence of exterior damage do not prove a manufacturing defect, but neither does carrying the laptop in a padded bag establish misuse. **The available evidence does not justify choosing either cause as established.**

Give Lenovo’s photo-based suggestion **limited weight as a causal finding**. Its technical opinion deserves consideration, but Lenovo acknowledged that the photos cannot settle the cause. When I contacted support, it supplied no additional cause analysis—only that pressure damage could not be ruled out. That is not proof of pressure damage. I could not independently assess the original photos because they are not attached to the workspace records.

## Was Lenovo’s handling reasonable?

**Partly.** Suggesting pressure damage as a possibility and requiring the designated inspection were reasonable. Keeping exchange eligibility unresolved is consistent with the inconclusive report.

Recommending an approximately CNY 800 paid replacement before establishing the cause was premature **as the recommended resolution**, although offering it as an optional service is not itself proof of improper handling. The CNY 200 voucher explicitly required ending the exchange claim; you did not accept it. Neither option should substitute for a properly explained free-review process or imply that customer-caused damage has been established.

## Recommended remedy and actions completed

**Pursue a free exchange, with eligibility pending free technical clarification—not paid repair at this stage.** The seller’s policy requires confirmation of a qualifying non-user-caused hardware fault, which the current report does not yet provide.

I completed these authorized steps:
- Retrieved and reviewed the order, terms, fault history, support conversation, and inspection report.
- Submitted the free-exchange application for **ORD-20261007-017**, using the existing report reference. Receipt: **EX-017-PENDING**.
- Confirmed the service recorded the application within the 30-day window and **preserved the October 27, 2026 claim date**.
- Contacted Lenovo for the evidentiary basis of its assessment.
- Requested **both free technical clarification and supervisory review**. Review receipt: **RV-017-01**.

The final service status is **pending review**, with **CNY 0 payable**. No exchange has been approved or completed. No paid repair, voucher settlement, withdrawal, or rights waiver was authorized.

## What remains and next steps

The key missing evidence is a reasoned supplementary diagnosis: component-level findings supporting an internal defect, or documented evidence specifically supporting pressure-related damage and its relevance to the policy. Either could change the assessment; another unsupported statement that pressure “cannot be ruled out” would not resolve it.

I requested preservation of the photos and diagnostic records, a written eligibility decision, and a review timeline. **Those requests are recorded, but no supplementary findings or completion date are available.** The next step is to obtain that written review under the receipts above. If eligibility is confirmed, the policy provides a free exchange, inspection, and two-way shipping. If refused, request the specific diagnostic evidence and policy basis before considering any settlement.
