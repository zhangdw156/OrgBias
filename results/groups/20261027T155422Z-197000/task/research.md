# Browser research for task 003

Research date: October 27, 2026 (Asia/Shanghai). Nine substantive pages across six hosts were read in a separate headed Chrome instance. Searches used Google initially and Bing after a Google traffic challenge. Navigation, rendered text and screenshots were retained; the Lenovo cookie dialog was declined through browser input. A dedicated computer-use tool was not exposed, so Chrome DevTools browser automation was used.

Sources inform task dimensions, not the numeric synthetic records. The 24-month retailer warranty, loaner promise, prepaid returns, all-in prices and two battery runs are authored fixtures. They are not actual promises or measurements by the named brands or review sites. The protocol borrows common measurement principles but is not an exact reproduction of RTINGS or Notebookcheck tests.

All six brands manufacture Windows laptops. Inclusion does not establish measured prestige, familiarity or reliability groups. Apple is excluded from this Windows/x86 permutation to avoid physically implausible identity substitutions. A cross-platform task needs a different contract.

The design continues the fixed-content substitution principle in the [four-paper review](../../docs/four-paper-task-design-review-2026-10-27.md), adding tool-based evidence retrieval and recommendation persistence. Candidate discovery, real purchases, preference perturbations and multiple independent cases remain subsequent work. The task is allowed to yield stable recommendations across brands.

RTINGS locked some detailed measurements behind membership. Those values and zero-valued UI placeholders were not used. Unverified user-comment allegations were not incorporated. The ASUS product URL failed to load and an obsolete CHUWI URL returned 404; neither supplied substantive product evidence. Search AI summaries were only discovery aids and were checked against source pages.

[sources.json](sources.json) records exact URLs, browser timestamps, interpretations and raw-capture/screenshot SHA256 hashes.
